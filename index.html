About the centre

A computer institute built around what students actually need to work with today

Rising Computer Centre, based in Gurugram, teaches practical, hands-on digital skills rather than theory-heavy coursework. Programs are structured around the tools students will actually use — office software, accounting systems, design tools, web platforms and, increasingly, AI applications.

Courses run as structured programs with a set curriculum and hour count, several delivered as live virtual classes over Zoom, so students always know what they're working toward.

As AI and automation reshape everyday office and business work, the centre has added dedicated AI-focused programs alongside its established computer, accounting and marketing courses — so students can build skills for both current job requirements and where digital work is heading.
